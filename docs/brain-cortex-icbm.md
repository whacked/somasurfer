Generated by `node tools/check-icbm-cortex.mjs --write-report` from the committed files, and checked against them on
every CI run. Do not edit; rebuild with `node tools/build-icbm-cortex.mjs --icbm <dir>`. The build's decisions are in
its header; this page is what the files say.

## The surface

- **Source:** `icbm152-2009c`, the ICBM 152 non-linear 2009c asymmetric template (MNI BIC permissive licence, its COPYING
  carried in `structures.json`).
- **Space:** MNI152 2009c asymmetric, natively. The surface was extracted in the template's own sform, so a coordinate in that space needs
  no mapping: `mapping.toMni152_2009cAsym` is `identity`, 0 mm.
- **Field:** GM + WM - CSF, level 0.4 (the CSF probability 0.3 level), inside the brain mask.
- **Low tier:** `brain/icbm152-2009c/cortex.lowres.bin`, 171,304 B, 143,989 B gzip. No full tier ships.

## The cerebellar leak

**The cerebrum cut is not validated.** The reference is the Z-Anatomy cerebellum carried by an affine whose held-out error is 8.55 mm at the deep grey nuclei and is extrapolated, unmeasured, at the cerebellum. Only counts deeper than that error are robust to it. No cerebellum label with a licence readable today exists for this template, so the cut is not validated.

Method: shipped low-tier vertices mapped into the brain-mesh frame by mniToMesh (registration/mni-to-zanatomy-2929e94.json) and tested for being inside the committed Z-Anatomy cerebellum (31 structures) by two-way ray parity; depth is the distance to the nearest cerebellar surface.

| shipped tier | vertices | inside the reference | deeper than 4 mm | deeper than 8.55 mm |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| left | 4,261 | 174 | 18 | 0 |
| right | 4,341 | 235 | 20 | 0 |

The reference's own placement: of its 70,315 vertices carried into MNI space,
54,584 land in tissue the cut removed, 9,872 in the kept cerebrum and
5,859 outside tissue. The ones in the kept cerebrum are leak, or registration error, or both;
this build cannot tell which.
